FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Answers based on Cord Staffing Solutions' recruitment services and process.

What services does Cord Staffing Solutions provide?

RPO, permanent staffing, executive search, talent sourcing, candidate profiling, talent mapping and recruitment support.

What is Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO)?

RPO is a recruitment model where Cord Staffing Solutions manages part or all of a client's recruitment process, from sourcing and screening to interview coordination and onboarding support.

Which industries do you serve?

IT, healthcare, accounting, insurance, construction, civil engineering, manufacturing, energy, retail, legal and other sectors.

Can you recruit for specialised or senior-level positions?

Yes. Executive search and specialised recruitment services help identify qualified professionals for senior, technical, specialised and hard-to-fill positions.

How do you source candidates?

Multiple sourcing channels include talent databases, ATS resources, professional networks, online platforms, targeted research and industry-specific talent pools.

Can you handle high-volume or bulk recruitment?

Yes. Scalable recruitment solutions can support bulk hiring and high-volume recruitment requirements while maintaining a structured process.

Can you work as an extension of our internal recruitment team?

Yes. The RPO model can work alongside existing HR and talent acquisition teams or operate as an extended recruitment function.

Why should we choose Cord Staffing Solutions?

With extensive recruitment experience, a broad talent network, industry expertise and a structured sourcing and screening process, Cord Staffing Solutions helps organisations improve hiring efficiency and access quality talent.
